The product

We built a browser extension that reads every chat message aloud while a streamer broadcasts. No more glancing at chat, no more missed comments. Sellers (e.g. Whatnot) and streamers (e.g. Twitch) stay focused on their audience and their product. The livestream TTS also works for online meetings such as Zoom and Google Meet.

The role

Figure out who gets the most value from this tool, how to reach them, and what makes them care. The core of the job is identifying and recruiting users who genuinely benefit from the product and are happy to leave honest reviews on the Chrome Web Store.

Compensation

Performance-based. Pay is tied to Chrome Web Store reviews and scaled to your level of experience. Additional tiers for bulk onboarding and B2B partnerships discussed during interview.

Who should apply

All backgrounds welcome. Previous software sales or knowledge of the creator economy is a plus. You need to be self-directed and comfortable with performance-based pay.
