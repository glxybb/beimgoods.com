Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. What we collect

Almost nothing. The Beim Pixel Project does not require an account, login, email address, or any personal information to use. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics services, or advertising scripts. No payment information is collected — the service is free.

2. What we store

When you claim a pixel, our database stores two things: the pixel color you chose and the time of the claim. No name, email, IP address, payment data, or device information is stored. Pixel data is automatically and permanently deleted after 24 hours.

3. Data retention

Pixel data is automatically deleted 24 hours after the claim via an automated expiration policy. We do not retain pixel data beyond this period.

4. Third-party services

Google Cloud / Firebase — for hosting our database and backend. Google's privacy policy applies to infrastructure operations. See Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

5. No tracking

The Beim Pixel Project does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, cookies, localStorage, or any form of user tracking. We do not build user profiles. We do not serve advertisements. We have no way to identify returning visitors.

6. Children's privacy

The Beim Pixel Project does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, no age-specific data exists in our systems.

7. Data security

All connections to The Beim Pixel Project use HTTPS encryption. Our database encrypts data at rest. Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized administrators.

8. Your rights

Since we store no personal information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Your pixel data is automatically deleted after 24 hours.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. Changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above. Continued use of The Beim Pixel Project after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, reach us at beimgoods.com.
