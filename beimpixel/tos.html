1. What beimpixel is

beimpixel is a pixel canvas at beimgoods.com/beimpixel. You can rent a single pixel on a 32×32 grid by choosing a color and paying $1.00 USD. Your chosen color will display on that pixel for 24 hours from the time of purchase.

2. Pixel leases

Each payment grants you a 24-hour lease on one pixel. After 24 hours, the pixel automatically reverts to the default image. Leases cannot be extended, transferred, or paused. There is no ownership of pixels — only temporary display rights.
