The Pixel Project @ ArtGalleryDemo

An 81×81 canvas, open to anyone. One dollar changes one pixel for 24 hours.

One pixel is a small act. Alone, each person pulls the canvas in a different direction. Over time, the painting drifts into noise.

But when a group coordinates, they compose something new, e.g. a painting, a message, a shared vision. What no individual can create alone, a collective can.

Alone, we make marks. Together, we build vision.
