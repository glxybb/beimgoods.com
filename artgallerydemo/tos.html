Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. What The Beim Pixel Project is

The Beim Pixel Project is a collaborative pixel canvas at beimgoods.com/artgallerydemo. Anyone can claim a single pixel on an 81×81 grid by choosing a color. Your chosen color will display on that pixel for 24 hours. No account, login, or payment is required.

2. Pixel claims

Each claim grants you a 24-hour display of your chosen color on one pixel. After 24 hours, the pixel automatically reverts to the default image. Claims cannot be extended, transferred, or paused. There is no ownership of pixels — only temporary display rights.

3. Occupied pixels

If a pixel is currently claimed by another user, you cannot claim that pixel until its 24-hour period expires. The site checks pixel availability before allowing a claim.

4. Acceptable use

The Beim Pixel Project is a shared canvas. While individual pixels are small, coordinated patterns are visible. You agree not to use pixels to display content that is hateful, illegal, harassing, or infringing. We reserve the right to remove any pixel at our discretion.
