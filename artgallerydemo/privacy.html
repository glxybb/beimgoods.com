Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. What we collect

Almost nothing. beimpixel does not require an account, login, email address, or any personal information to use. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics services, or advertising scripts.

2. Payment data

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Your credit card number, billing address, and other payment details are submitted directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. We receive only a payment confirmation ID from Stripe, which we store solely for transaction records.

3. What we store

When you rent a pixel, our database stores three things: the pixel color you chose, the time of purchase, and a Stripe payment reference ID. No name, email, IP address, or device information is stored. Pixel data is automatically and permanently deleted after 24 hours.

4. Data retention

Pixel data is automatically deleted 24 hours after purchase via an automated expiration policy. We do not retain pixel data beyond this period. Stripe retains payment records according to their own privacy policy and legal obligations.

5. Third-party services

beimpixel uses two third-party services:

Stripe — for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment information. See stripe.com/privacy.

Google Cloud / Firebase — for hosting our database and backend. Google's privacy policy applies to infrastructure operations. See Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

6. No tracking

beimpixel does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, cookies, localStorage, or any form of user tracking. We do not build user profiles. We do not serve advertisements. We have no way to identify returning visitors.

7. Children's privacy

beimpixel does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, no age-specific data exists in our systems.

8. Data security

All connections to beimpixel use HTTPS encryption. Our database encrypts data at rest. Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized administrators with multi-factor authentication.

9. Your rights

Since we store no personal information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Your pixel data is automatically deleted after 24 hours. For Stripe payment records, contact Stripe directly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. Changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above. Continued use of beimpixel after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, reach us at beimgoods.com.
